Web Development

This course is designed to provide Multimedia Engineering students with an understanding of and hands-on experience in front-end web development. Over the course of several weeks, students will learn everything from the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to the advanced use of libraries and frameworks such as React.js. The course combines theory with practice, including individual and team projects, to ensure that students can apply what they have learned in real-world contexts.
